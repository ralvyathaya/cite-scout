## Source review implementation

The owner's approved [assistance boundary](2026-10-27-source-review-assistance.md) is implemented in the local-only review server, form and evaluator. It does not add a provider, dependency, public endpoint or database table.

Implementation commit: `87f0a48` (`fix(evaluation): preserve gold during assisted source review (U4)`). It remains local; no push or deployment was performed.

- 444 source occurrences are represented by 171 groups: 109 calibration ownership groups, one calibration missing-content group, 60 held-out ownership groups and one held-out missing-content group. Grouping retains each original record/field reference and never crosses partitions or ownership contexts. Checked content, when supplied, has its own content-specific grouping.
- Calibration proposals use deterministic domain rules and explicit missing-content abstention; they are labelled as rules, not claimed to be an AI inference. No page contents are invented from a URL/title. The supplied corpus has no checked page contents.
- A deterministic sample of calibration ownership groups, with representation of each proposed class and all unresolved cases, requires individual spot-checks before bulk acceptance of the remaining determinate calibration ownership proposals. A mismatch blocks bulk acceptance; correcting a sample after bulk approval removes those bulk decisions. The held-out groups and content-type groups are never covered by that bulk operation.
- Held-out choices start blank and expose no proposal. Identical evidence can be judged once for its explicitly listed group; repeated mapped fields are not independent statistical samples. Spot-checks of visible calibration proposals are assisted, not independent gold.
- `human-labels.json` retains the original human values. Group decisions live in separate `source-review.json`, with reviewer/time, individual versus batch acceptance, assisted versus independent mode, packet hash, group/protocol hash and exact field coverage. Group decisions fill only originally blank fields. Existing different human choices are preserved and displayed separately, with a difference count in the report; their disagreement does not block filling the group's remaining targets.
- Save checks cover local host/origin/token, exact choices and fields, stale composite versions, concurrent writes, explicit completion and frozen files. Completion remains a human action. The agent did not accept a proposal, enter a label, complete a review, freeze a real lock or inspect classifier decisions.
- Freeze includes the sidecar hash. Scoring excludes every assisted field from overall, held-out/calibration and engine accuracy summaries; it reports assisted and independently reviewed group counts separately. A result with assisted exclusions is explicitly named `partially_human_reviewed_real` (or synthetic). Checked/missing source-content counts are reported overall and by partition, independently of annotation completion. Shared missing-content type groups offer only an explicit `unknown` abstention; they cannot transfer an unsupported category such as editorial into independent gold. Scoring without the locked sidecar or after changing its provenance fails. The legacy strict path still requires all human values when no sidecar is supplied. `pilotGatePassed` remains false in every mode.

All 341 existing human choices and the packet were byte-checked unchanged when restarting the form. Their SHA-256 values remain `eb68c8eef465c8a8e4a466f1ff249c4f288b3ed5b0b38ef1dbab0c0a3a524c4e` and `ba6f27ee54053d97cd413596893c295c5f1d16c41e35c2ce25fd599b4d977825`.
